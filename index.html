Spear & Mace Dojo

Online duel (LAN or internet)

Quick match: you and your friend pick the same ruleset, type the same room code and press Connect. That is all. Internet is only used to let your two pages find each other; the game then connects directly between you, so it also works on the same Wi-Fi. Use a code others will not guess (5+ letters).

Movement

WASD move · Space jump (press it again in the air to glide in the elytra) · double-tap W or hold Shift to sprint · the mouse looks (arrow keys if your browser blocks mouse capture).

Hotbar

1 Lunge III spear · 2 Density V mace · 3 wind charge · 4 firework · 5 elytra or the chestplate you took off · 6 sword · 7 axe · 8 ender pearl · 9 golden carrot. Scroll or number keys switch. Everything attacks with 3 blocks of reach. These are the defaults; change them in the inventory and key binds.

Mouse

Left click attacks (spear jab, mace swing). Right click uses the item: hold it with the spear to charge, throw wind charges, fire rockets while gliding, swap elytra and chestplate.

Elytra mace

You start in the elytra. Jump off the sky (T puts you high up already gliding), press Space to open the elytra again, rocket with 4, dive, then select slot 5 and right-click to take the elytra off (Java needs it off to smash). Press 2 and hit.

Lunge swap

The server judges your attack with the item you held at the end of the last tick. Hold the wind charge until its bar fills (5 ticks), then press 1 and click in the same tick. The jab fires with the wind charge’s bar. The wind charge’s 10-tick cooldown must be over.

Reach swap

Hold the spear, then press the mace (or any weapon) and click right after. The server keeps the old item’s attributes for exactly one tick (50 ms), so the hit reaches 4.5 blocks as if you still held the spear. The crosshair turns green when the dummy is in that range. Slow motion and extra swap grace in the inventory settings help you practise.

Double binds

In the key binds tab every action takes two inputs, keys or mouse buttons, and one input can be bound to several actions. It then triggers all of them in the same tick, slot selection first. Example: put Slot 2 and Attack on the same mouse button and one click is a reach swap from whatever you hold; put Slot 1 and Attack on another and one click is a lunge swap from the wind charge.

Attribute swap helper

In the key binds tab, bind a button or key to swap to a slot and attack in the same tick. With the preset, left click reach-swaps to the mace from whatever you hold and right click lunge-swaps to the spear (it replaces normal right click use).

Axe duel

Diamond sword (7 damage, 12.5-tick cooldown) and a diamond axe with Sharpness V (12 damage, 20-tick cooldown, disables shields). Diamond armour has Protection IV, no knockback resistance and no Feather Falling. 20 HP, no totems. A stack of five golden apples sits in slot 3 and a shield in your offhand: hold right click for 1.6 s to eat one apple (+4 hunger, Regeneration II 5 s, Absorption I 2 min); you can eat when full. The bot has a shield in its offhand and uses both weapons: the axe to open and to break your shield (a hit disables it for 5 s), then the sword to combo you while it is down. Bot attacks need a clear line, so walls block them.

Shield

Hold right click with a shield (your main hand if it holds a shield, otherwise the offhand) to block. It needs 5 ticks to come up, blocks all melee damage from the front 180° with no knockback to you (the attacker is pushed back a little), and you move at 20% speed while it is up. An axe hit is blocked but disables the shield for 5 s. Falls, pearls and the void ignore it. The hand poses follow your screenshots: idle the shield sits low in the corner (only its top edge shows), blocking it is raised in front of you. In the axe duel the bot carries one too: it raises it when you are about to hit (it needs 5 ticks to come up), and your axe disables it for 5 s.

Neth pot duel

Netherite sword and armour (Protection IV, Feather Falling IV), 20 HP and splash potions: Instant Health II, Speed and Strength II. The bot has the same kit and plays it like a pot player: it buffs with Speed and Strength while you are far away, heals with pots when you cannot punish it (low on health it sprint-jumps away to get room to pot), crit-chains with jumps, punish-crits you (a p-crit: your hit launches it, and it hits back on the way down), rushes you while you hold a pot, D-taps when your totem pops and you hold no other, eats steak when it is safe and throws XP bottles to mend its armour (it wears down like yours and breaks, so it protects less). Swapping to a pot resets your sword cooldown, so pot right after you have swung. Its first hit of a fresh fight lands a few ticks late (a human reaction time), so you can get the first strike; follow-up hits in a combo are not delayed. Sprint hits launch you to the full vertical knockback even in netherite (only the sideways knockback is reduced), which is what p-crits use.

Pearls and totems

The ender pearl and the totem of undying are in the inventory pool. Right click throws a pearl: it teleports you to where it lands or hits (5 damage, cut to 1 by Feather Falling IV in the mace duel; 1 s cooldown), and a wind charge that touches or bursts next to your own pearl catches it: the pearl teleports you right there and the burst boosts you (any other pearl is just pushed). A totem saves you from fatal damage only while it is in your offhand or main hand (offhand first) and is used up. A pop sets you to half a heart, clears nothing else, and gives Absorption II (4 hearts, 5 s), Regeneration II (45 s) and Fire Resistance (40 s), shown top right. Sword duel: 1 totem each. Mace duel: 3 each, one in your offhand and two in slots 7 and 8, so press F to put the next one in your offhand.

Food and hunger

Hold right click with a golden carrot for 1.6 s to eat it: +6 hunger and +14.4 saturation. You move at 20% speed while eating, and you cannot eat with a full hunger bar. Steak gives +8 hunger and +12.8 saturation and comes as a stack of 16. Hunger starts full and saturation starts at 5 (the Java respawn value), and both drain like Minecraft: sprinting costs 0.1 exhaustion per block, a jump 0.05 (0.2 while sprinting), a landed hit 0.1 and taking damage 0.1, and every 4 exhaustion costs 1 saturation (or 1 hunger once saturation is gone). Health regenerates from food: at full hunger with saturation left you heal about half a heart every half second, at 18+ hunger 1 HP every 4 s, and each HP healed costs 6 exhaustion. At 6 hunger or less you cannot sprint. Lunges cost 3 each (saturation goes first) and need 7+ hunger. Hunger is restored at the start of every round, and with R in free practice. The bot has hunger too in the mace duel: its lunges cost 3 and need 7+, so when it runs low it stops lunging and eats a golden carrot (1.6 s, standing still) as soon as you give it a gap.

Armour durability and XP bottles (Java rules)

Netherite armour base durability is helmet 407, chestplate 592, leggings 555, boots 481. With Unbreaking III counted tool-style (×4) that is 1,628 / 2,368 / 2,220 / 1,924, which you can switch on in settings; by default the HUD shows the base numbers. Every hit you take that armour protects against wears each worn piece by max(1, damage ÷ 4 rounded down). In Java mode, Unbreaking III makes each point land only 70% of the time (60% + 40% ÷ 4). Fall damage, pearls and the void do not wear armour. A broken piece disappears. Armour is a real item now: click a worn piece to pick it up, click an armour slot with a piece on your cursor to wear it, right-click a piece in a slot to wear it, shift-click to send it to or from your inventory. Bottles o’ Enchanting: right-click throws one (64 per stack). It shatters into 3–11 XP split into orbs that fly to you; with Mending each XP point repairs 2 durability on a random damaged worn piece. Neth pot starts with two stacks in your inventory. Hold right-click to keep throwing. Four slots left of the hotbar show your armour durability. Walking, head turning, eating, gliding and spear swings are animated on the opponent’s model and sent online. The spear jab swings like the sword and axe. Holding right-click: the spear rises, points out sideways while the charge is strong, then, as knockback (110 ticks) and then damage (175 ticks) run out, it turns back down to normal. Enchanted gear (weapons, armour, elytra, potions and XP bottles) shows the animated purple glint, which stays on in performance mode too. Online, your opponent sees and hears your bottle throws, the shatter and the orbs flying into you, and hears your armour break and sees the piece disappear from your character (also when you take a piece off). The durability numbers themselves stay private, as in Java.

Feather Falling IV

Duel armor has Feather Falling IV. In the mace duel it stacks with Protection IV (capped at 80%), so a pearl does 1 damage and a long fall only a few points. In the sword duel it cuts fall damage by 48%. The bot can never pop a totem from its own pearl or from landing after one.

Bot D-tap

From Hard up, the bot knows the D-tap. When you pop a totem and have no other totem in your hands, it rushes you and hits again as soon as your damage immunity ends. Master and Grandmaster hold the sword and swap to the axe for a full-power hit. Get a totem back into your offhand (F) fast, or keep a spare in your hand.

Loud hits

Your mace hits and the totems you pop on the bot always play at full volume, with no distance falloff (they ignore the volume setting, unless sound is Off). Your own totem pops play much quieter, so they do not drown the rest out.

Saved loadouts

Your hotbar and offhand arrangement is remembered separately for free practice, the sword duel and the mace duel. Rearrange it with E; double-click clears a slot. “Reset this loadout” in the inventory restores the default for the current duel.

Offhand

Set it in the inventory (E); F swaps hands. Right click tries your main hand first and only uses the offhand if the main-hand item has no use. Attacks always use the main hand.

Other keys

R reset dummy · T sky start · Y arena · G dummy moves or stands · B Wind Burst on/off · V dummy shield on/off (an axe hit disables it for 1 s, then use the mace) · H hitboxes · P third person · E inventory. T, Y, G, B and V only work in free practice.

Every action takes two inputs, keys or mouse buttons. Click a box, then press a key or click a mouse button (Backspace clears it, Esc cancels). The same input can be bound to several actions and triggers all of them in the same tick, slot selection first. Double-bound inputs show in yellow.

Attribute swap helper

Bind a mouse button or key to swap to a slot and attack (or use) in the same tick, so reach swaps and lunge swaps take a single input. It replaces that input’s normal actions. With “swap back” you return to the item you held, ready for the next one.
